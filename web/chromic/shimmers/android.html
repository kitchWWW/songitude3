Shimmers in the Field

On Android, with the Songitude app

On Android, Chromic's walks play in Songitude, the free app that powers them.

Download Songitude Free on Google Play
  1. Download SongitudeIt's free. Tap the button above.
  2. Open itTap Continue and allow your location, so the sounds can follow you as you walk.
  3. Tap "Shimmers in the Field"It's in the list of soundwalks, sorted nearest first, so in the park it should be at or near the top.
  4. Press playThe walk opens on a map and downloads. When it's ready, press the round play button and start wandering.

How to listen

Enable location services, put on headphones, and press play. The GPS-triggered audio does the rest—no fixed route, no need to look at the phone.

Headphones give the fullest experience. Your phone can go back in your pocket; the music keeps playing with the screen off.
