CHANGES IN THIS BUILD — 1.0 (11)

Guideline 2.1(a), the unresponsive button. We found it: the Report screen. In Settings > Report,
the "Report <walk name>" and "Report <artist name>" rows were disabled whenever no soundwalk was
loaded, because there was nothing to name. On the review device that is the ordinary state, since
almost every walk is tied to real coordinates elsewhere and none loads by default — so two buttons
appeared on screen and did nothing when tapped. They are now hidden when there is nothing to name
rather than greyed out, and "Report an issue" is always available. The map follows the same rule:
play and skip no longer appear when no walk is loaded.

Guideline 5.1.1(iv), the permission request. The screen shown before the system location prompt no
longer uses the word "Enable". The button that advances now reads "Continue", and the screen
explains only what the app does with location. The other choice is "Not now", which dismisses
without requesting anything.

DEVICE SUPPORT

Songitude is an iPhone app (TARGETED_DEVICE_FAMILY = 1). There is no iPad build or interface. It
requires GPS hardware (UIRequiredDeviceCapabilities: gps, location-services) because the experience
is a person walking through a real place while the audio responds to their position. Wi-Fi-only
iPads have no GPS and cannot install it. We ask that review be done on an iPhone, the only device
the app is designed for and the only one where its core behaviour can be observed.

HOW IT WORKS

Songitude plays music composed on a map. As the listener walks, GPS decides which audio layers
are audible. Location is used only on the device, in the moment, to choose what plays; it is never
uploaded, stored or shared, and is requested only while a walk is playing. No account is needed.

TO TEST

Allow location and pick a walk. Audio is tied to real coordinates, so use a simulated location near
a walk, or choose one from "Listen From Anywhere" — those arrange themselves around wherever you
stand and work from any location, including inside the review facility. Try "Lament for Zoid":
open it, press play, and walk a few metres to hear the layers change.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT — GUIDELINE 1.2

Audio cannot be created or uploaded from inside the app. It is listen-only, downloading finished
walks from our public catalog; walks are composed and published on our website. We declare UGC
because those walks are authored by composers other than us.

1. FILTERING BEFORE POSTING. Publishing is invite-only, gated by a server-side allowlist of Google
accounts we approve by hand; an account not on the list cannot publish. The list holds only
composers we know personally. Before publishing, a composer must confirm they hold full rights to
every piece of audio; publishing is blocked until they do, and both their timestamp and our
server's receipt time are recorded.

2. REPORTING. Settings > Report offers "Report [walk]", "Report [artist]" and "Report an issue",
with the current walk and artist filled in. Each emails us directly, read by a person
normally the same day. Also documented at https://songitude.com/support/index.html

3. REMOVAL AND BLOCKING. Every walk is owned by an identified Google account, so it traces to a
person. We can delete a walk immediately and remove a composer from the allowlist permanently.

4. PUBLISHED CONTACT. The in-app report forms reach us by email. Our address is also published on
our support page and in our privacy policy.

PRIVACY

The app collects nothing: no accounts, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, and location never leaves
the device. Policy: https://songitude.com/privacy/index.html
